Fish and Shrimp in our Tank

Last month Amy brought 6 neon tetra fish to our class. She instructed us how to feed the fish and shrimps with their own food. The children immediately brought up a good question, '' Can fish and shrimps smell their food? How do they know which food is theirs"?. The class has been studying on that topic through books and discussions. Please feel free to talk about it at home with your child.

Visiting experts Valeria and Alic

On November 7th, Valeria and Alic came to our class. Valeria, the fish-egg expert, read a book that she wrote specifically for our children-The Red Egg That Became a Blue Fish (红蛋变蓝鱼). Connecting to our Hair Study last year, Valeria and Alic talked about how DNA gently guides each cell to work together to make a baby fish. Following the wonderful book, the children were led in an origami activity that showed how life begins from a single cell and grows more complex as cells divide (1 to 2, 2 to 4, and so on).
