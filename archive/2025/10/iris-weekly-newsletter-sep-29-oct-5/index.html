Making Lantern

The Mid-Autumn Festival is around the corner, our class kicked off our activities this week. and we are so excited to welcome our first volunteers—Alicia Chen, Alicia Dai, and Adam to join us in making lanterns. During this activity, the children designed their own patterns for the lanterns and practiced their fine motor skills in different ways. For example, they drew straight lines by connecting two points, and to make their lanterns more colorful and detailed, they experimented with zigzag lines, curly lines, circles, waves, and more. Afterwards, they practiced hand-eye coordination by cutting along straight lines. With the volunteers’ support, the children completed their lanterns. Each lantern is unique and will be displayed in our courtyard to celebrate our 中秋节. 

 Just would like to give you a heads up that the children are going to make our own moon cake at school next week.
