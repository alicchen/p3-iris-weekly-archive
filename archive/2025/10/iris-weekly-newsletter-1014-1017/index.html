Shrimp

On Tuesday we welcomed the new members to our Iris class, 3 fresh-water shrimps. Thank you Amy for buying them and preparing the new water environment for them. Their size now is like finger nail. According to Amy, they are going to grow to be 2 centimeters when fully grown up in a year. We also learned a big word together, Crustacea, 甲壳类, which is the family name that shrimps belong to. The children took the close observation when Amy moved them from her small tank to our big tank. We hope our shrimps live a happy life in our class.

On Wednesday the teacher brought some frozen shrimps to the class for the children to have a hands on experience of how a shrimp looks like and feels like. They identified shrimp's head, eyes, antenna, tail, scale (shell), legs. The children also noticed the shrimp's body can form a C shape and a straight line shape because of the flexible shelf segments. Many said that they have shrimp at home but never have seen them with head and shelf. Some are very curious and cannot wait to touch and feel the structure and texture, and some think they are gross. The teacher cooked the shrimps later and let the children compare them. 

   The first question is brought up by Lillian: 'Why the cooked one became red?'. "Why do you think they changed color? The teacher asked. 

 Cosmo and Victoria believed that it is the stove, the fire changed the color and Cleo said it's the heat changed it. Then teacher asked, 'why pasta did not change color after I cooked it?"

 Francis and Juniper immediately associate this phenomenon with something that we learned last year with the Hair Study, "I think It's the DNA, when you cook the shrimp, the DNA told the body to change the color."

Skyler commented: " this is an interesting experiment."

  While we were fascinated and puzzled some children suggested we can check Chatgpt or ask Siri. We encourage the children to bring this question home and discuss this with Mom and Dad. We hope to they bring back your discussion when they come back to school in the next weeks.
