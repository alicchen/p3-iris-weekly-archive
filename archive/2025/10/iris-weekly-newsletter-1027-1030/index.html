Observational Drawing of Shrimp

With our new shrimps in our fish tank and hand-on experience of studying a real shrimp, in the past two weeks the children have been working on their observational drawings of shrimp. We are so glad to see that here are lots of details in the children's drawings, 'I draw head and body (segments), tail, antennae, legs, eyes on stalks'. Some children even created a color pattern for their shrimp.
