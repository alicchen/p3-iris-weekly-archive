Discussion of leaf

In our meeting, the teacher showed the children some freshly picked leaves. The children then compared them with the leaves they had collected two weeks ago. They noticed that some of their leaves had dried out. A few children shared that the leaves were “too old” and had “died,” which is why they fell to the ground.

Our children asked some questions from their observations, such as:

  • “How do leaves 'drink' water?”

  • “Can leaves 'drink' milk?”

  • Do leaves have a mouth?

  • What's at the very bottom of the leaf?

  • What do leaves "eat"? 

 We are so amazed by the children’s curiosity, and we look forward to exploring these questions and discovering the answers together.

Leaf Creations

Our children were inspired by the book Leaf Man, a story about leaves that travel wherever the wind blows them. To spark their imagination, we explored the books together and wondered what else leaves could become. We asked, "Where might leaves travel? Who might it meet?" Our children shared that, leaves will go to the ground, the ocean, the garden, the river, and Washington DC, San Diego Zoo and so on. The children also discovered that leaves could turn into a person, a turtle, a fish, a chicken, ducks, a shark, and many other creative shapes. We encouraged them to observe each leaf’s color, shape, size, and texture, helping them imagine how these natural materials could be rearranged into new forms. With their imaginations activated, they created their own Leaf Man or leaf creature, each child telling a unique story/ character.
