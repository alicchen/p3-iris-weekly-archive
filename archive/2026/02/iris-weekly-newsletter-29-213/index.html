3D Sea Creature Making

The field trip to the Aquarium of the Bay has deeply sparked the children curiosity and the sense of exploration. This couple of weeks the children used the recycle materials to make the sea creatures such as Stingray, Puffer Fish, Clown Fish, Jellyfish, Lobster, Crab, Shark, Sea Turtle, Octopus and Sea Otter.

 We would like to thank Irene and Gabriel who set up 'an ocean-themed background' in our classroom so the children's ocean creatures could be beautifully displayed near it.

Show and Tell

Juniper kicked off our Show-and-Tell this Tuesday. He introduced a rocket ship that he made at home. The class asked many questions after Juju's presentation, "How did you make it?", "Why do you like rocket ship?", "Did anyone help you?", "How much do you know about rocket rocketship?", "What is the most challenging part when you made the rocket ship?"... Juju answered all the questions one by one. The most impressive part is when he talked about how he overcame the difficulty when his rocket ship was 'busted' the night right before the show-and-tell, and he tried hard to fixed it by using tapes to keep the cardboard pieces together.

 We are looking forward to seeing more children share in the future.
