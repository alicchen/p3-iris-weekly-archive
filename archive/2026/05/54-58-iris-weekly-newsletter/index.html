Show and Tell

Ari did a show and tell about an airplane he made with popsicle sticks. He also made an airport for his plane to land on. He spoke in Mandarin almost the entire time. At one point, he even kindly reminded a friend to raise their hand before speaking.

Some of the questions and answers were as follows:

  • “How did you put the sticks together?” Ari answered, “I used a hot glue gun.”
  • “Did your parents help you with the glue gun?” Ari answered, “First, my mom had to put my brother to sleep so he wouldn’t distract us. Then my mom showed me how to use the hot glue gun. I watched her, and then I learned how to use it myself.”
  • “What kind of airplane is this?”
    Ari answered, “It’s a special kind that can do performances and tricks. I have a toy airplane like this at home.”

Skyler did a show and tell about a firetruck he made with Lego. He also used a lot of Mandarin during his presentation. The class asked the following questions:

  • “Which side is the front?”
  • “How long did it take you to build it?” Skyler answered, “Five minutes.”
  • “Where is the door on this firetruck?” Skyler answered, “There is no door.”
  • “Why did you want to do a show and tell about a firetruck?” Skyler answered, “Because I like firetrucks.”
  • “What else have you made with your Lego at home?” Skyler answered, “I made other types of cars at home.”

The class also discussed how firetrucks help people, such as putting out fires, taking people to the emergency room, and helping rescue cats or dogs stuck in trees.
