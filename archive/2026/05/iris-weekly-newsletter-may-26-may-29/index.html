Colombian Culture Introduction

Naomi’s mom, Caroline, visited our classroom to share about Colombian culture in a fun and hands-on way. The children had the opportunity to explore different items connected to Colombia through their senses, including coffee beans, flowers, “emeralds,” and music.

Through smelling, touching, hearing, and tasting, the children will engage in a meaningful cultural experience while learning about the natural resources and traditions of Colombia. We were excited for this interactive experience and thankful to Caroline for bringing this special learning opportunity to our class!
Show And Tell

This week, our children had another round of Show and Tell. We were so happy to see how much they have learned from one another. Many children remembered to introduce themselves before beginning their presentations, showing growing confidence and communication skills.

The children also asked meaningful and thoughtful questions, such as, “What kinds of materials did you use?” and “Why did you choose those materials?” It was wonderful to see their curiosity, listening skills, and engagement during their friends’ presentations.

Naomi showed us a book she made by herself. She drew some avocado seeds, water, clouds, pizza, numbers, a mountain, and lots of hearts. She used a complete sentence to introduce her book, for example: 这是, (This is ....)Our children asked her some questions: How long did you take to make the book? Why did you draw some clouds and stars in the book? She said it takes one day to finish the book, and she likes clouds and stars.
Atlas showed us a wooden car that he made at home. He shared that he followed instructions to build the car. The car comes in different colors, such as purple and green, but blue is his favorite. Atlas also showed us how his wooden car moves. The children were very interested in his wooden car and asked thoughtful questions about how it was made and how it worked.

Anya showed us a clay unicorn that she made with her mom. She introduced the different features of a unicorn, such as its eyes, legs, body, and head. Our friends asked her why she wanted to make a unicorn for Show and Tell. Anya shared that she loves unicorns very much and enjoys creating artwork with her mom.

Oliver showed us a mouth organ that he made. He introduced how he created it using hot glue, cardboard, and straws of different lengths. Oliver also demonstrated how to make sounds by blowing across the straws without touching them with his mouth. The class was very interested in exploring the different sounds made by blowing through the straws.

Locke shared with us a tooth that fell out last week during the vacation. He was very proud that he lost his first tooth and shared that he received ten dollars from the tooth fairy. The children were curious and asked Locke how he was able to get the tooth back from the tooth fairy. Locke explained that his mom sent a message to the tooth fairy so he could borrow the tooth back to show the class.
