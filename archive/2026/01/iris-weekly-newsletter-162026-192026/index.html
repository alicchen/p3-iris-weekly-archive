Reconnect with friends

Our children were very excited to see each other. During the meeting time,  they eagerly shared stories with their friends about the activities they enjoyed during winter break. Some talked about spending time with their families, going to the park, having a playdate with friends, or finding an animal's footprint on the snow. These conversations helped the children practice expressing their ideas in the group, listening to their friends, and building connections with one another. 

When we place leaves under the microscope

While playing in the big tree playground, the children noticed that the leaves of a Perilla plant looked very special. They observed what looked like tiny fur on the surface of the leaves. Some children wondered if it might be dirt or even poop. Then we placed the leaves under a microscope. The children were very excited to see the tiny hairs and the many veins inside the leaves. Some children shared that the veins looked like a spider web. Veins became so much bigger, like a small tree. They also noticed many small dots on the leaves. The children shared their thoughts:

“叶子被虫子咬，有一百万个点。” (“The leaves were bitten by caterpillars, there are one million dots!”)
Others wondered if the dots might be ants’ eggs; Leaves were stung by a bee

Later, a child picked a leaf from the lemon tree and held it under the sunlight. The dots became even more visible. Children had  another questions:

  • Do all leaves have small dots?

  • Why do leaves have these dots?
