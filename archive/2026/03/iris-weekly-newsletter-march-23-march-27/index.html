The children explored the tiny seeds by touching them and observing them with magnifiers. They sorted the seeds by size, shape, and color, and also created observational drawings of the seeds.
The peas grew taller

After the beans sprouted, we transplanted them into the planter box. After two weeks of growth, we noticed that the pea shoots had grown much taller. Some children measured the plants and found that they had reached 19 cm. Max also observed that there were some white spots on the leaves. We discovered that the pea shoots have tendrils. We learned that tendrils help the plant “grab” onto support structures, allowing it to climb and reach more sunlight. To support the pea shoots, we collected many sticks, and the children helped place them into the soil. Christie also worked with the children to build a small trellis, preparing a structure for the tendrils to climb.
