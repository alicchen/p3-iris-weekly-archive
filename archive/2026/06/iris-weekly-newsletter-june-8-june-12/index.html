Iris Weekly Newsletter June 8- June 12

Dear Iris Families,

It is hard to believe that this is the last week of preschool. Over the past two years, we have enjoyed watching the children grow, learn, and develop their creativity and confidence.

This year, the children explored two topics through hands-on experiences, investigations, and play. They shared their ideas, asked thoughtful questions, worked together with friends, and discovered new ways of understanding the world around them.

We are so proud of all they have accomplished and grateful for the support and partnership of our families throughout this journey. Thank you for being part of our Iris community.

We wish all of our children and families a wonderful summer and all the best as they begin their next adventure.

With gratitude,

Iris teachers
