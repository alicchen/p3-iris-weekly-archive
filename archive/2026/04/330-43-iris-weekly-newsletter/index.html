Squid

Inspired by the book-INK, the teacher brought squid to the classroom this week. As the teacher dissect the squid, we found the ink sac, eye balls, mouth, eight legs and 2 tentacles, and one piece of 'transparent bone' which is called Pen or Gladius. The children touched the Pen and bent it and noticed that it's softer and more flexible than our human bones.

Under the microscope we observed the beak inside squid's mouth, there are two main pieces-upper and lower. This parrot-like jaw is hard and bites like scissors. We also observed the eyeball spherical lens. They are hard and like pearls.  Under microscope we can clearly see the suckers on the leg, which are used for grabbing prey and anchoring.

Show and Tell

Victoria did a show-and-tell about riding Carrousel. She showed the class all the different places that she rode carrousel. Some are in the city and some riding experience is outside the city, like in San Jose.


    Within all the Carrousel riding experiences Victoria said her favorite two animals to ride on are Zebra and Unicorn. 


   Tori also shared that after sitting on the "animal" you need to fasten the seat belt for safety. 

 

  Many children told Victoria that they like riding carrousel too.

Lillian showed us a postcard she made after inspired by her dad giving her mom a flower. 


She explained how she made the postcard. First finding a piece of paper, and then folded it with all corners to form a pocket. After that  she went to search in her toy box and picked a mini stuffy, white bear. Carefully she squished the fluffy inside the pocket. At last, she made a handle for the postcard, like the stem of a flower.


One child asked. " How long did it take you to finish?" "2 minutes" Lillian answered. The children wowed, "That's fast!"
