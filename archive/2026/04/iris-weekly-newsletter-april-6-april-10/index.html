Celebrating Earth Month

Thank you, Alicia Chen and Joyce, for coming to celebrate Earth Month with our children. Alicia and Joyce read books with us, The Lorax and Crab Cake, which highlight the important message of protecting our environment. When trees are cut down, animals lose their homes, and the air and water become polluted. Crab Cake is more than just a dish. It symbolizes care, love in cooking, and respect for nature.


Alicia and Joyce also introduced a trash-sorting activity with the children. We learned what items can be recycled (such as aluminum cans, cardboard or shoe boxes, glass jars, and paper), which can be turned into new products. We also learned what can be composted (such as food), which returns to the earth and helps new plants grow. In addition, we discussed items that can be reused (such as glass jars, cardboard or shoe boxes, plastic takeout containers, and used toys), as well as the importance of reducing waste.


At the end, the children have a coloring activity to learn more ways to protect our Earth. For example, we can use less electricity, water, plastic, and paper. We can also choose eco-friendly transportation, such as bicycles or public transit, protect animals, and plant trees.


The children also exchanged gently used toys, and each child had the opportunity to participate in a lucky draw using a recycled bag.
Field Trip to the Heal Project Farm

Thank you to all the volunteers who came to help. We took a bus through the tunnel, saw the ocean, and arrived at the Heal Project Farm.

Our children participated in a sensory tour around the farm. They smelled thyme plants, tasted calendula and honey, and touched the soil and earthworms. They also learned that farmers sometimes use potatoes as food for earthworms. In addition, the children picked strawberries, turnips, and green onions. The farmer also showed us how to plant peas in a way that helps protect them from birds.

This field trip provided hands-on experiences where children could see, touch, smell, and explore. It deepened their understanding of where food comes from and helped them build a meaningful connection to the real world.
